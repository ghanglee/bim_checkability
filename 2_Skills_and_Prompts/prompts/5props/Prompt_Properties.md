# Prompt  
#   
You are evaluating building-regulation requirement sentences for automated (BIM) compliance checking. This is a standalone task. Use ONLY the definitions and examples below. Assume no prior context and no previously agreed answers; do not infer how any sentence is "supposed" to be labeled, and do not look for patterns across sentences. Judge each sentence on its own. For each requirement, assign all five properties: 1) Sentential Completeness (Complete/Incomplete): Complete if a grammatically whole sentence with subject+predicate; Incomplete if a fragment, heading, or dangling clause. Complete: "Corridors shall be at least 2400 mm wide." Incomplete: "…not less than 1100 mm." 2) Normative Completeness (Complete/Incomplete): Complete if the deontic action and all needed values/conditions/threshold are present; Incomplete if obligation, target, or threshold is missing. Complete: "Guards shall be at least 1.0 m high." Incomplete: "Guards shall be provided." 3) Objectivity (Objective/Subjective): Objective if judged against a measurable, unambiguous criterion; Subjective if it relies on judgment words. Objective: "Doors shall be at least 850 mm wide." Subjective: "Adequate ventilation shall be provided." 4) Self-Contained / Referential Completeness (Yes/No): Yes if nothing beyond the sentence is needed; No if it refers out to another clause, table, figure, or undefined term. Yes: "Ramps shall not exceed a slope of 1:12." No: "Ramps shall comply with Table 3.2." 5) Atomicity (Atomic/Compound/N/A): Atomic = one requirement (incl. one carrying preconditions/ exceptions, or alternatives joined by OR); Compound = two or more obligations joined by AND that should be split; N/A = not a requirement (definition, scope, heading). Atomic: "Stairs shall have handrails on both sides." Compound: "Stairs shall have handrails and be at least 1000 mm wide." N/A: "This section applies to hospitals." Output one CSV row per input: id, Sentential, Normative, Objectivity, Self_Contained, Atomicity, Rationale (one short clause). Classify every row; skip none.  

Rename the files as LLM model name_version_evaluation subject[CheckableOnly | Property]_method[1Agent | 5Agent | Mo5E]_serial number(optional). Example: Gemini_3.1Pro_CheckableOnly_1Agent.csv.
